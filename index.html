02 · Prognostic modelling

LASSO-Cox model performance

A penalized Cox proportional hazards model was fitted for exploratory feature selection and prognostic modelling.

The test-set C-index indicates modest discrimination. The difference between training and test performance suggests limited generalizability may be present. These results do not establish clinical validity or clinical utility.

04 · Analytical workflow

Methods at a glance

Expression and survival data were processed and matched before survival analysis and model development.

STEP 01Data acquisitionGSE37642 expression matrix and clinical survival annotations.
STEP 02PreprocessingData cleaning, survival endpoint parsing and sample matching.
STEP 03Survival analysisKaplan–Meier estimation and descriptive outcome summaries.
STEP 04Model developmentLASSO-Cox feature selection and train/test C-index evaluation.

Interpretation

This project is an educational and hypothesis-generating analysis of a public AML cohort. Model coefficients and performance estimates are specific to this dataset and modelling procedure.

Limitations & next steps

Independent external validation, assessment of calibration, robustness testing and evaluation in clinically relevant settings are required before any clinical interpretation or application.
